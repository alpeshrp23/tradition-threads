More than clothing

Every culture
has a story.

Tradition Threads is a student-focused project designed to make traditional attire easier to discover and more affordable. Students often need special outfits for cultural events, school activities, festivals, and weddings.

Our goal is to help students celebrate their own heritage, learn about other cultures, and take part in community events with confidence. We plan to compare suppliers, listen to student preferences, and offer options for different budgets.
